Privacy Policy

Updated: Sep 17, 2026

Aliaksei Khutarshchykau ("us", "we", or "our") operates the photopages.app website and the Photo Pages mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Because our Service processes media locally on your device, we do not require account registration or personal data collection to operate. By using the Service, you agree to the processing of technical diagnostic data and direct communications in accordance with this policy.

Information Collection And Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Types of Data Collected

PERSONAL DATA

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or payment details. Your photos, documents, and output files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

If you contact us directly, we will only use the information you provide to address and resolve your inquiry.

DIAGNOSTIC & USAGE DATA (CRASH DATA)

We may collect non-personal usage and diagnostic data solely to improve the functionality, stability, and performance of our Service. This data does not identify you personally and is not used for tracking or marketing purposes.

This data may include:

  • The type of device and operating system version you are using
  • App performance statistics and error logs (Crash Data)
  • Anonymous device identifiers generated for diagnostic purposes

We use third-party diagnostic services, such as Bugsnag, to collect Crash Data and technical logs. This helps us identify bugs, analyze crashes, and ensure the app operates reliably.

TRACKING & COOKIES DATA

We do not use tracking technologies or cookies to track user activity across third-party applications or websites, and we do not participate in targeted advertising.

On our website (photopages.app), third-party services like Google Analytics may set standard technical cookies to collect non-personal aggregate data about website traffic. You can manage or disable cookie preferences in your browser settings at any time.

EMBEDDED CONTENT FROM OTHER WEBSITES

Articles or pages on our website may include embedded content (e.g., videos or external links). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor has visited the other website directly. These external sites may collect data about you or use cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Use of Data

We use provided information and technical data exclusively for internal operational and support purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To monitor app performance, stability, and system health
  • To detect, prevent, and diagnose technical issues and crashes
  • To address, investigate, and resolve user inquiries and support requests

Transfer Of Data

Your information, including non-personal diagnostic data, may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state, province, country or other governmental jurisdiction where data protection laws may differ.

If you are located outside Poland and choose to use our Service, please note that operational data is processed in accordance with European Union data protection standards (GDPR). Your use of the Service represents your agreement to that transfer.

Disclosure Of Data

LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

We may disclose information in good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation
  • Protect and defend our rights or property
  • Prevent or investigate potential wrongdoing in connection with the Service
  • Protect against legal liability

Security Of Data

The security of your data is important to us. Because your media and files remain on your local device, you retain full control over your personal content. While we use commercially acceptable means to protect technical diagnostic data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can take prompt action to remove such information from our records.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the Service following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions?

If you have any questions or privacy concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via our Contact Page or through Email Support inside the Photo Pages mobile app.
