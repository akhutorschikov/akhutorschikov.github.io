Pricing

Updated: Sep 17, 2026

Availability and Use

To start using the app right away, follow the link: Get Started

The app is available for free on the App Store. You can explore all internal functionality without restrictions; however, to obtain clean production-ready outputs, a commercial use license, and other benefits a paid option is required. New users can take advantage of our 7-day free trial on subscription plans, or choose Lifetime Access with all future updates included.

Why Subscription & Lifetime?

Our choice to offer both subscription and lifetime models is driven by our commitment to providing you with flexibility without compromising quality. Subscriptions empower us to continually invest in the app, ensuring it remains responsive and equipped with the latest capabilities. At the same time, our Lifetime option serves professionals who prefer a single upfront investment over recurring payments.

This combined approach enables us to offer dedicated support, swift updates, and a seamless workflow. Your purchase directly sustains the app and contributes to its ongoing enhancement, allowing us to consistently deliver top-notch performance and meet your evolving needs.

Pricing Options

The prices displayed in this section are indicative and may vary based on your location and local currency.

The app offers flexible Monthly and Annual plans, along with a one-time Lifetime purchase. Opting for an Annual plan provides a significant discount compared to Monthly billing. All subscriptions include a 7-day free trial, and every option grants the exact same level of access and commercial licensing:

  • Monthly - $7.99 / month
  • Annual - $59.99 / year (~$4.99/month, 37% discount)
  • Lifetime — $99.99 (One-time payment, no recurring charges)

What is Included?

To preview upcoming app features please follow the link: Upcoming Features

Every option grants complete access to all current features. All future updates will be fully covered by both subscription plans and the Lifetime purchase. To learn more about our roadmap and capabilities, please visit our Features page.

Managing Your Subscription

Subscriptions are managed and processed securely through the App Store via your Apple ID. If you wish to manage or cancel your subscription, you can do so at any time directly through your iOS Settings. For step-by-step instructions, please refer to Apple's official documentation.

Canceling ensures you won't be charged for subsequent billing cycles, while full access remains active until the end of the current paid period.
